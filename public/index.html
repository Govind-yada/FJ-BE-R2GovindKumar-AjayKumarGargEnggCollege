How Anomaly Detection Works
📊
Z-Score Analysis
Compares each transaction to your historical average for that category. Flags transactions more than 2 standard deviations from the norm.
📅
Frequency Analysis
Detects if you're transacting more often than usual in a category — e.g. 20 food orders this month vs your average of 8.
💸
Large Transaction Alert
Flags any single transaction that is more than 3× your average for that category and above ₹5,000.
